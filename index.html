Connect the puck to change these

The puck's LED and buzzer (if fitted) use the same velocity-loss cut-off as the app (set on the Train screen). Reps lifted while your phone isn't connected are saved on the puck and imported the next time you connect. After 10 minutes still, the puck sleeps; tap it or move the bar to wake it.

Import a week by pasting

In your coach's sheet, open the week's tab, select everything (Ctrl/⌘ + A), copy, and paste below. A simple table with columns Day, Exercise, Sets, Reps, Load, %, RPE works too, as does a CSV file.

Edit week

One line per exercise. Prescription: 3x5 @ 160, 1x3 @ 80%, 4x4 rpe 6, or several joined with commas: 1x1 @ 170, 3x3 @ 155 rpe 6. % uses the 1RMs above.
